## No 01 HOUSE — dill and peppercorn

Cold, briny and mouth-puckering. Cucumber-forward, with black peppercorn and a long dill
finish. Bottled in 375 ml. Keep refrigerated after opening.

## No 02 DIRTY — martini brine

Built for the glass. Pour 15 ml into a stirred martini, or 30 ml if you like it filthy.
Sharp, saline, olive-adjacent. Also very good in a bloody mary.

## No 03 SMOKE — smoked chilli

Smoked chilli and bay over a garlic-dill base. Splash it into a michelada, or use it to
deglaze a pan after searing pork.

## In the kitchen

Brine a chicken overnight. Loosen a mayonnaise. Cut through a rich sauce. Macerate red onion
for ten minutes and put it on everything.
