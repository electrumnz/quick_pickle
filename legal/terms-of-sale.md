## 6. If something is wrong — your remedies

These are the CGA's remedies, not ours, and we do not shorten them.

**Where the failure can be remedied (CGA s 18(2)):** you may require us to remedy it within a
reasonable time. Under **s 19** we may do that by repair, by replacement with goods of identical
type, or — where repair cannot reasonably be expected, which is the normal case for a food product —
**by refunding what you paid**. If we refuse, neglect or fail to remedy within a reasonable time, you
may have the failure remedied elsewhere and recover the reasonable cost from us, or reject the goods.

**Where the failure cannot be remedied, or is "of a substantial character" (CGA ss 18(3), 21):** you
may **reject the goods** and choose a **refund or a replacement**, or keep them and claim
compensation for the drop in value. A failure is of a substantial character if, among other things,
a reasonable consumer fully acquainted with it would not have bought the goods, or the goods **are
not of acceptable quality because they are unsafe** (s 21(d)).

**In addition (CGA s 18(4)):** you may claim damages for any other loss or damage that was
reasonably foreseeable as liable to result from the failure.

**Rejecting goods.** Under **s 22** you reject by telling us you are rejecting and why. Under **s 20**
the right to reject is lost if it is not exercised within a **reasonable time** — which for a
perishable food product, judged by "the type of goods" and "the length of time for which it is
reasonable for them to be used" (s 20(2)), is short. **Tell us as soon as you notice.** The right is
also lost if the goods have been disposed of, lost or destroyed while not with us, or were damaged
after delivery for reasons unrelated to their condition at supply.

**Practically:** email **[ORDERS@DOMAIN]** with your order number, what is wrong, and a photo if you
can. For anything that looks like a food-safety issue — an off smell, a swollen or leaking cap, a
broken seal, a foreign object, or illness after drinking — **stop using the product, keep the bottle
and the cap, and tell us immediately.** We need the batch code, and we may have legal obligations of
our own (see `food-safety-checklist.md`).

The CGA does not give a remedy where you simply changed your mind, misused the product, or were told
about the issue before you bought. In particular:

- **Sediment, cloudiness and separation are normal.** It is a vegetable brine, not a soft drink.
  Batch-to-batch variation in colour and intensity is inherent to the product and is disclosed here,
  on the product page and on the label.
- **Refrigerate after opening and use within the period stated on the label.** Ignoring that is
  misuse.
- **We do not offer change-of-mind returns on food.** That is a food-safety position, not a limit on
  your CGA rights, and it is explained in `shipping-returns.md`.

## 8. There is no cooling-off period for online orders — and we are telling you that honestly

New Zealand's statutory cancellation right applies to **"uninvited direct sale agreements"**. Under
**Fair Trading Act 1986, s 36K**, that means an agreement made through negotiations **in each other's
presence in your home or workplace** where you did not invite the supplier, or **by telephone** where
you did not invite the call, and where the price is over $100.

**An order you place on our website is neither.** So there is no statutory 5-working-day cooling-off
period, and we do not offer a voluntary one for food. If you want to change or cancel an order, ask
us before it is dispatched and we will do our best (§9).

We say this plainly because implying a right that does not exist, or denying one that does, is itself
a breach of **FTA s 13(i)**.
