## The nature of what you are buying

HALF SOUR is a **food**. It is a bottled vegetable brine with a limited life, it is heavy, it is
glass, and once it has left our hands and been sitting on someone's doorstep we cannot put it back on
the shelf. Everything on this page follows from those four facts.

**Store it as the label says.** Where the label says _Keep refrigerated after opening_, that is not
advice — it is the storage condition the product's date mark depends on. Under the Food Standards
Code, a **best-before date** is the date up to which the food remains fully marketable **if it stays
in an intact package and is stored in accordance with the storage conditions on the label**
(Standard 1.2.5—2). A **use-by date** is the date after which the food should not be consumed for
health or safety reasons on the same assumptions — and **Standard 1.2.5—4** provides simply: _"A food
must not be sold after its use-by date."_ We will never knowingly ship you something at or past its
date.

## 4. Change of mind

**We do not accept change-of-mind returns on food.** Once a food product has left our control we
cannot verify how it has been stored, and we will not resell it to someone else. This is a food-safety
decision under our Food Act obligations, not a limit on your legal rights.

To be precise about what that does and does not mean:

- It **does not** affect any Consumer Guarantees Act right. Faulty, unsafe, mis-described, wrongly
  delivered or late goods are dealt with in §§2–3, whatever the reason you are unhappy.
- There **is no statutory cooling-off period** for an order placed on a website in New Zealand. The
  cancellation right in the Fair Trading Act applies to **uninvited direct sale agreements** — sales
  negotiated in person in your home or workplace, or by an uninvited phone call, over $100
  (**FTA s 36K**). A website order is neither. We are not withholding a right; the right does not
  exist, and saying otherwise would itself breach **FTA s 13(i)**.
- **Before dispatch, just ask.** If it has not been packed we will change or cancel it and refund in
  full.

## 5. Refunds

- Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually clear in **[X working days]**,
  which is the bank's timeframe rather than ours.
- A refund for a faulty product includes what you paid for shipping on that product.
- If you rejected the goods under **CGA s 18(3)**, **you** choose refund or replacement, not us —
  **CGA s 23(1)**. And **s 23(3)** is explicit that the obligation to refund "cannot be satisfied by
  permitting the consumer to acquire goods from the supplier": a store credit is not a refund.
- Where the nature of the failure means the goods cannot be returned without significant cost to you,
  **CGA s 22(2)(a)** puts the cost of collecting them on us. A leaking bottle of brine is exactly
  that case.
- We do not issue credit notes in place of a refund where the Act gives you a refund.
